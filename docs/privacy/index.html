PostPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

PostPilot is a private social media planning tool used to create, schedule and publish posts to Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that its operator manages, and to view the performance of those posts.

Information we access

  • Facebook Page and Instagram account names and IDs that you connect.
  • Access tokens needed to publish on your behalf.
  • Posts published through PostPilot and their engagement metrics (reach, views, likes, comments, shares).

How we use it

This information is used only to publish content you approve and to show you analytics about your own Pages. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Data is stored securely and kept only as long as needed to provide the service.

Data deletion

You can remove PostPilot's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations. To request deletion of all stored data, email claudecode20k@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Your data will be deleted within 30 days.
